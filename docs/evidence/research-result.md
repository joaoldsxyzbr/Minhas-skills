# Aster versus Boreal at 500,000 monthly operations

**Recommend Boreal on cost: 18 credits/month versus Aster’s 20 credits/month, a saving of 2 credits/month (10% of Aster’s cost).** These calculations use the most recent effective prices in the supplied fictional packet, both effective October 1, 2026. They are not present-day verification of real services. [S2, S3]

## Cost calculation

Assume exactly 500,000 billable operations per month and only the charges stated in the packet.

| Vendor | Applicable pricing | Calculation | Monthly total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aster | Base 5 credits; 0.03 credits per 1,000 operations, effective 2026-10-01 | 5 + (500,000 / 1,000 × 0.03) = 5 + 15 | **20 credits** [S2] |
| Boreal | 10 credits includes 300,000 operations; excess 0.04 credits per 1,000, effective 2026-10-01 | 10 + ((500,000 − 300,000) / 1,000 × 0.04) = 10 + 8 | **18 credits** [S3] |

Savings: 20 − 18 = **2 credits/month**; 2 / 20 × 100 = **10%**. [S2, S3; calculation]

Aster’s September price would produce 5 + (500,000 / 1,000 × 0.02) = 15 credits, but its metered rate was replaced by S2 on October 1. The community article published October 20 repeats the superseded September price; its newer publication date does not make that price effective again. The October 22 mirror repeats the same article verbatim. These two articles form one derivative evidence chain, not independent corroboration of current pricing. [S1, S2, S4, S5]

## Privacy and uptime

- **Privacy:** Boreal states 30-day usage-log retention, but customer-configurable deletion is not stated. Aster’s retention is unstated. Missing information is not evidence of zero retention or stronger privacy, and Boreal’s disclosed retention alone does not establish superior privacy. The packet cannot support an overall privacy ranking. [S1, S2, S3]
